Real-Time Movement Analysis and Automated Fitness Assessment using Computer Vision

01 Introduction & Overview
Computer Vision

The AI-Powered Fitness Assessment Platform is a modern computer-vision application designed to assist coaches, sports academies, and fitness trainers in conducting objective physical evaluations.

  • Replaces subjective manual counting with real-time pose estimation.
  • Extracts 17 full-body keypoints from a standard webcam video feed.
  • Calculates joint angles and movement displacement via vector mathematics.
  • Unifies live movement analysis, athlete management, and historical progress.
02 Problem Statement
Challenges Addressed

Traditional fitness testing in academies and schools relies heavily on manual stopwatches, physical observation, and paper scorecards.

  • Observer Bias: Human fatigue causes missed repetitions and inconsistent judging.
  • No Joint Angle Verification: Incomplete repetitions (e.g. shallow sit-ups) get falsely counted.
  • Data Fragmentation: Paper-based records make cohort tracking and progress analysis cumbersome.
  • Accessibility: High-end motion capture labs are prohibitively expensive for grassroots sports.

🏃‍♂️ Eight Fitness Exercise Modules
Physical Battery
⬆️
Vertical Jump
Displacement (cm)
Tracks ankle vertical Y-displacement from the standing baseline.
➡️
Broad Jump
Displacement (cm)
Measures horizontal takeoff-to-landing ankle X-displacement.
🔄
Sit-ups
Repetitions (Count)
Calculates hip angle (Shoulder-Hip-Knee); counts full UP/DOWN cycles.
🖐️
Sit & Reach
Reach (cm)
Evaluates maximum forward wrist reach beyond toe baseline.
⚡
Sprint Test
Time (Seconds)
Tracks ankle line-crossing triggers with precision digital timer.
🏐
Medicine Ball
Distance (cm)
Measures upper-body explosive power via wrist release proxy tracking.
🔁
Shuttle Run
Crossings (Laps)
Counts directional agility boundary crossings via hip coordinate.
⏱️
Endurance Run
Laps Counted
Monitors stamina cadence through completed round-trip crossings.
🧠 Biomechanical Movement Analysis
Computer Vision Math
Joint Angle Formula (Vector Dot Product)
θ = arccos( (BA · BC) / (|BA| × |BC|) )
For 3 points A (Shoulder), B (Hip), C (Knee), vectors BA and BC compute the precise flexion/extension angle.
